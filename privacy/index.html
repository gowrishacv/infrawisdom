Scope and summary

Bhagavad Gita Kannada (the “App”) is provided by Infrawisdom, operated by Gowrisha C. Vishwa Kumar (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy applies to the App. It explains how the App handles information when you use it.

The Bhagavad Gita text, Kannada explanations, search, bookmarks, and appearance settings work on your device. We do not operate a developer-controlled server for the App and the App does not send personal information to us.

Information stored on your device

Feature Information Purpose
BookmarksSaved chapter and verse identifiersShow your saved verses

Search terms are used only while you search. They are not stored or transmitted by the App.

Optional Sign in with Apple

You can use every reading feature without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, the App requests your name and email address through Apple’s authentication interface. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal email address.

After a successful sign-in, the App stores the Apple user identifier and, if returned by Apple, your display name and email in local device storage. The current version does not send those values to a developer-controlled server, create an online account, or retain Apple authentication tokens. When a previously signed-in App launches, it may ask Apple to check the local credential’s status.

Apple’s handling of Sign in with Apple is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Network use and third parties

Gita content is included in the App bundle. Reading, searching, bookmarking, and changing appearance do not require an internet connection.

The App contains no developer-controlled network endpoint and no advertising, analytics, social, crash-reporting, or other third-party SDK. Sign in with Apple is an Apple system service and may require an internet connection to complete sign-in or verify credential status.

Sharing

If you tap the Share button, the App prepares the selected verse and Kannada explanation for Apple’s system share sheet. The App does not receive the destination or a copy of what you share. Information you choose to send is then handled by the app or service you select and its privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising or marketing, track you across apps or websites, or receive your bookmarks, searches, appearance choice, name, email address, or Apple user identifier on a developer-controlled server.

Your choices and deletion

  • 01You can use all reading features without an account.
  • 02Choose Sign Out on This Device in Settings to remove the locally stored Apple user identifier, name, and email.
  • 03Remove bookmarks individually in the App, or uninstall the App to remove its local data. Manage any device backups separately in Apple device settings.
  • 04To revoke Sign in with Apple authorization, manage the App in your Apple Account’s Sign in with Apple settings.

Because we do not maintain a remote user account or server copy of this information, there is no developer-held profile for us to access or delete.

Children’s privacy and changes

The App is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children, and does not knowingly transmit personal information to us.

If the App’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before or when the change takes effect.
